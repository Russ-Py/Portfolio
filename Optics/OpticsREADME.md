## HBT and GRA Experiments

Contained in this folder is documentation regarding my faithful recreation of the Hanbury Brown-Twiss and Grangier, Rogers, and Aspect experiments (and two modified variations). These experiments were pivotal in the acceptance of the quantum theory of light. Both experiments leverage simultaneous photon detection to show that beams of light are not continuous.

This folder also holds a PowerPoint presentation that I gave to a group of peers to recap this procedure, my single-photon-detector data, and a technical report written for the project.
